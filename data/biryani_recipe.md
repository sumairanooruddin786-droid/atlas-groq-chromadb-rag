# Chicken Biryani at Home

This recipe feeds six people and has nothing to do with machine learning. It is included so that the embedding map shows
a document about a completely different subject.

## Ingredients

One kilogram of chicken, three cups of basmati rice, four onions, four tomatoes, one cup of yogurt, ginger garlic paste,
green chillies, fresh mint and coriander, biryani masala, turmeric, red chilli powder, salt, a pinch of saffron soaked in milk
and half a cup of oil or ghee.

## Marinade

Mix the chicken with yogurt, ginger garlic paste, biryani masala, turmeric, chilli powder and salt. Let it rest for at
least two hours, or overnight in the fridge, so the spices soak into the meat.

## Cooking the masala

Fry sliced onions in oil until deep golden, then take half of them out for the topping. Add the marinated chicken and the
tomatoes and cook on medium heat until the oil separates and the chicken is tender, about thirty minutes.

## Rice and layering

Soak the rice for thirty minutes and boil it in salted water with whole spices until it is seventy percent cooked, then drain.
Spread half of the rice over the chicken, add mint, coriander, fried onions and a few spoons of saffron milk, and then
add the rest of the rice.

## Dum

Seal the pot with a tight lid or dough and cook on the lowest heat for twenty five minutes. Let it rest for ten minutes, mix gently
from the bottom and serve with raita and salad.
